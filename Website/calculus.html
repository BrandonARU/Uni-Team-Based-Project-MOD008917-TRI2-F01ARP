Calculus

Welcome to calculus — the part of maths that everyone fears… until they realise it’s just about change.

There are two main parts:

  • Differentiation (rate of change)
  • Integration (area under a graph)

Differentiation

Example:

y = x²

→ dy/dx = 2x

This tells you how steep the graph is at any point.

Basically: how fast something is changing. Like checking how quickly your motivation disappears during homework.


Integration

Integration works backwards.

It finds the total area under a curve by adding up tiny pieces.

Think of it like counting grains of sand… but with less beach and more maths.


AQA Tips

  • Learn the power rule
  • Be careful with signs
  • Show working clearly

Calculus looks scary at first… but so does every maths topic right before it makes sense.
